# sendflowrs

A small website for sending someone a bouquet of animated ASCII-art flowers that bloom when they open the link.

## Who it is for

- **Senders:** anyone wanting to make someone's day in under a minute, usually on a phone, with no account.
- **Recipients:** whoever gets the link, often in a chat app, with no idea what the site is. They need nothing but the link.

## What success looks like

The recipient feels something when the flowers open. The site itself recedes.

## Voice

Warm and quiet, like a handwritten card. Lowercase-friendly and never salesy. Errors stay gentle ("these flowers have wilted") and still say what to do next.

## Constraints

- Lightweight: under 30,000 bytes of gzipped JavaScript outside the QR library, and no font downloads.
- No accounts, analytics or payments.
- Reduced motion, screen readers and dark mode are first-class.
- It is also a public portfolio project.
